Evenings, Monday to Thursday

A beautiful big hall with room to move

Evening classes run at the Old Vaaltonians Club, with plenty of space to spread out. Each evening starts with a 15-minute Circuit Fit warm-up at 17:00, then an hour of Pilates, Yoga or Circuit Fit.

Bring your mat and a water bottle. Sam has spare mats if you forget.

Mornings, Monday, Wednesday, Friday

Live online, from home

No rushing and no travelling. Roll out your mat, open your laptop and move with Sam in real time. Live instruction and guidance, suitable for all fitness levels.

All you need is a yoga mat, a small space and an internet connection.
